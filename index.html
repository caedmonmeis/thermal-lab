At 0 mph you see thermals only. With wind, thermals still push scent up or down the slope on top of the wind, and they matter most in bottoms and behind ridges where the wind is blocked.

Rule based model: sun angle on each slope heats or cools the ground, air rises upslope when warm and drains downslope when cool, and wind is slowed in bottoms and behind ridges. Clear sky assumed. Confirm with a wind checker on the ground.
